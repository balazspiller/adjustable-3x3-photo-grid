How to use:
  • Click "Add Images" to select photos - you can do this multiple times until all slots are filled
  • Use the + and − buttons to add or remove columns (right) and rows (bottom) - from 1x1 up to 5x5
  • Choose Reorder to swap images, or Position to drag the crop and scroll over an image to zoom
  • Drag the horizontal or vertical grid lines to adjust cell sizes
  • Drag the bottom handle to change the canvas aspect ratio; hold Shift to resize only the bottom row
  • Set your desired export width (higher = better quality)
  • Click "Download Collage" to save your high-resolution creation

This tool works entirely in your browser - no images are uploaded to any server.
